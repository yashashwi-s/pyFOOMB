## Rerun order

1. Start with Crl1 SCC and MCC, which have both defined fed-batch runs and tabulated rates for the exact two clones. Confirm which protocol details carry over, then replay the reported exponential runs.
2. Make the batch and feed substrate identities, phase-specific kinetics, start volume, feed composition, pump limits, and stop conditions explicit scenario inputs. Keep published endpoints distinct from engineering constraints.
3. Rerun the eleven schedules on each sourced, compatible model under a common score and constraints **within that case**. Save new provenance and results separately from the October comparison. Compare final titre, total product, and elapsed time whenever termination depends on biomass or volume.

## Completed Crl1 reruns

Nieto-Taype et al., Fed-batch cultures, explicitly sets the endpoint at about 100 g/L biomass. Both clones now have eleven optimised profiles using the referenced glycerol/glucose protocol, with 40 g/L glycerol and a 2 L batch charge. The script starts feed at the glycerol-depletion mass balance, uses Table 1 rate points and transfers biomass and liquid volume. The score is activity formed during fed-batch. Pre-existing batch activity was omitted as an unknown constant within each clone.

Engineering assumptions include a 4 L ceiling, 40 h maximum feed time, 2 L/h pump ceiling, 0.1 g/L inoculum, a glycerol yield of 0.49 from the protocol paper and the generic glucose growth curve. SCC is fitted as growth-associated with a zero intercept and MCC uses a saturating curve. The rate fit is held flat above the upper fitted growth range used for the sweep (0.147 h⁻¹). The simulation stops at the first of biomass target, volume ceiling and time limit. The results use a finite-budget search and tighter replay, not experimental validation. The MCC winner reaches the time limit at 99.67 g/L.

From VIIth-sem, run `python paper_benchmark/optimize_crl1.py SCC`, repeat with `MCC`, then run `python paper_benchmark/plot_crl1.py`. The deck includes both plots. The other seven original rows remain pending source or model work.
