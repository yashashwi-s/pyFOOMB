Quick update on the fed-batch feed optimisation work:

- Took the 9 qp-mu datasets and simulated a full batch + fed-batch run for each.
- Tried several feed profiles F(t) - constant, exponential, linear-ramp, two-stage,
  pulsed, feedback-control, and exponential+linear - and optimised each one's
  parameters using differential evolution (a global, gradient-free optimiser;
  same family pyFOOMB itself uses for parameter estimation).
- Attaching the linear-ramp results (F(t) = F0 + k*t) for all 9 products - it's
  simple to run in practice and comes within a few % of the best strategy for
  most of them.

Happy to walk through the rest (bell-shaped ones prefer exponential feeding,
by a bigger margin) whenever you have time.
